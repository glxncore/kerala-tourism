Living Traditions

Experience the Culture

Unbroken lineages of classical arts, temple percussion symphonies, and ancient martial prowess.

Theyyam (തെയ്യം)

In North Malabar's sacred groves, mortals transcend into living deities through blood-red facial makeup, towering headdresses, and dance over flaming embers.

Kathakali (കഥകളി)

The celebrated classical dance-drama combining elaborate paccha makeup, stylized facial mudras, and epic stories drawn from the Ramayana and Mahabharata.

Kalaripayattu (കളരിപ്പയറ്റ്)

Regarded as the mother of all martial arts, practiced inside subterranean pits with flexible swords (urumi), wooden staffs, and animal postures.

Natural Cycles

Kerala, This Season

Kerala experiences two majestic monsoon rhythms: the south-west Edavappathi (June to August) and north-east Thulavarsham (October to November). Highlands become wrapped in dramatic cloud corridors while waterfalls reach their peak majesty.

Traveler's Note:

Monsoon is the most revered period for Ayurvedic healing as the skin pores naturally open in humid, cool atmospheric conditions.

Practical Wisdom

Good to Know

Essential practical tips to travel comfortably, safely, and respectfully across Kerala.

🚆

Getting Around

Coastal districts are linked by frequent scenic trains and state KSRTC buses. Government ferry boats in Alappuzha and Kochi cost pennies and offer authentic commuter views.

🌦

Weather & Attire

Light breathable cottons for coastal zones. Light woolens for Munnar and Wayanad evenings. Always carry a reliable umbrella or rain shell.

🍛

Dining Etiquette

Traditional Sadya is eaten with your right hand. When finished, folding the plantain leaf toward yourself signals enjoyment and gratitude to the host.

🏛

Temple Etiquette

Traditional attire (dhoti/mundu for men, sarees/salwars for women) is mandatory in ancient sanctums like Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple.

Sustainable Footprint

Travel gently. Honor the land.

Kerala's Responsible Tourism Mission empowers local village panchayats, coir artisans, and organic farmers. When you choose community homestays and village canoes, your journey directly sustains the heritage you came to admire.
